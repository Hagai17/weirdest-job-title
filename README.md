A tiny Python program that writes one weird job title to `/output/entry.txt`: a single line of at most 60 characters naming a job almost nobody has heard of, yet one you can picture a real person doing — a professional mourner, hired to grieve at the funerals of strangers. The title was picked by drafting twenty candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — a genuinely weird job title first, then stranger than ordinary work, a funny twist that lands on first read, a title that still sounds like a real job rather than word salad, and short enough to take in at a glance. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
